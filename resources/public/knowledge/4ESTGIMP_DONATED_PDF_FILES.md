## What Was Donated

4estGimp personally donated a PDF export of the **first 400 entries (sheets/rows)** from their comprehensive Fallout 4 patch and compatibility database. This is a printout/export of their famous Zoho Sheet, capturing their most important and widely-used patch records.

These files represent **years of hands-on testing, patch creation, and community collaboration** by one of Fallout 4's most trusted modding contributors.

---

## Why This Matters

The 4estGimp patch database is considered an essential reference for:
- Resolving mod conflicts in large load orders
- Finding compatibility patches before they're needed
- Understanding which mod combinations cause crashes or bugs
- Identifying the correct load order placement for complex mods

Having the first 400 sheets absorbed directly into Mossy's brain means **Mossy can answer patch and compatibility questions without needing to send users elsewhere first.**

The donated PDF covers the first 400 rows of 4estGimp's patch sheet, which includes:

- Munitions integration patches for weapon damage and ammo systems
- Keyword compatibility patches for weapon modding frameworks

### NPC & Faction Patches
- Raider Overhaul compatibility patches

- Faction behavior and AI package conflict resolution

### Crafting & Economy
- Equipment and Crafting Overhaul (ECO) integration patches

### Gameplay System Patches
- General bug fixes and feature-level integration patches

### Load Order Guidance
- Recommended placement for complex patch chains
- Plugin dependency notes
