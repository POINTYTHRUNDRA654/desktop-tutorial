## The Real, Teachable Pattern

The single most useful insight from this donation, confirmed by repetition across dozens of real rows: for nearly every popular mod they use, 4estGimp maintains **their own pair of compatibility files** — an ESL-compacted/cleaned "Edit" version of the plugin, plus a "CIS INNR ini" Complex Item Sorter injector patch that hooks the mod's items into their own crafting/sort categories. This repeatable two-file template is how a prolific patch author scales compatibility maintenance across a huge, evolving mod list, rather than writing bespoke one-off patches. See [4estGimp's Fallout 4 Database Overview](./4ESTGIMP_FALLOUT4_DATABASE.md) for real named examples.

Also worth noting: many entries appear in parallel "FOOG"/"FONG" pairs (Old-Gen/Next-Gen console-update variants) — 4estGimp maintains both builds side by side for many mods, a real pattern worth knowing for anyone managing a modlist across the FO4 Next-Gen update split.
