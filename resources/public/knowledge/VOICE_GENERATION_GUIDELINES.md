# AI Voice Generation Guidelines for Fallout 4 Mods

## ⚠️ Quality Reality Check

**AI-generated voices are for prototyping and testing only.** For competitive Nexus mods that stand out, real voice actors significantly outperform AI-generated dialogue. This guide helps you decide which approach is right for your mod.

---

## AI Voice Generation: When to Use

### ✅ Good Use Cases

- **Prototyping & Testing**: Quickly test dialogue flow and timing without committing to voice acting
- **Minor NPCs**: Background characters, one-line dialogue, environmental flavor text
- **Modding Workshops**: Educational demos showing how dialogue systems work
- **Placeholder Dialogue**: Temporary voice lines while you work with voice actors
- **Low-Stakes Content**: Non-story dialogue, repeating barks, automated responses
- **Rapid Iteration**: Test multiple dialogue options quickly before finalizing scripts

### ❌ Poor Use Cases

- **Main Quest Dialogue**: Story-critical NPCs need human emotional depth
- **Companion Followers**: Players bond with companions—AI voices break immersion
- **Major Characters**: Sell-point NPCs that define your mod
- **Emotional Scenes**: Drama, tragedy, romance require real acting
- **Large Dialogue Volumes**: More than 30-40 lines per character

## Real Voice Actors: Why It Matters

### 🎭 Why Voice Actors Win

| Factor | AI Generated | Real Voice Actors |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| **Emotional Depth** | Flat, robotic | Natural, convincing |
| **Character Distinct** | Generic profiles | Unique personalities |
| **Immersion** | Breaks player focus | Enhances storytelling |
| **Mod Popularity** | Viewed as "cheap" | Praised as "professional" |
| **Fallout Series Feel** | Doesn't match game quality | Matches Bethesda standards |
| **Repeat Listenings** | Annoying at iteration 5+ | Engaging every playthrough |

### 💰 Budget-Friendly Voice Acting

- **Fiverr/Upwork**: $25-200 per character for indie quality
- **Local Drama/Comedy Schools**: Students eager for portfolio work
- **YouTube/TikTok Creators**: Thousands of voice actors seeking opportunities
- **Mod Community**: Voice actors actively collaborate within modding community
- **Remote Recording**: Modern mics ($50-150) = professional indie recordings

### 📊 Mod Competition Reality

Nexus Mods with **professional voice acting**:
- 2-5x higher endorsements
- Better user reviews mentioning "professional quality"
- Featured more frequently by Nexus staff
- Become reference mods in the community

## Platform 21 (AI Voice Generation) Use Strategy

### Recommended Workflow

1. **Draft Phase**: Use AI to test all dialogue options (fast iteration)
2. **Script Finalization**: Lock down final dialogue with your AI tests
3. **Production Phase**: Hire voice actors for all major dialogue
4. **Polish Phase**: Use AI for minor ambient voices, one-liners, background NPCs

### Features You Get

- **Voice Cloning**: Match character voice profiles or tone
- **Batch Dialogue**: Generate multiple lines for testing efficiency
- **Lip-Sync Auto-Gen**: Professional mouth animation data
- **Emotion Control**: Neutral → Raspy → Robotic variations
- **Quick Turnaround**: Test updates in hours instead of weeks

## How to Get Professional Voice Acting

### Option 1: Community Voice Actors
- Join Fallout 4 modding Discord servers
- Post in r/FalloutMods seeking voice talent
- Many volunteers want portfolio credits

### Option 2: Freelance Platforms
- **Fiverr**: Search "Fallout 4 voice acting"
- **Upwork**: Post project for competitive bidding
- **Voice123**: Specialized voice actor marketplace
- **Casting Call Club**: Free community platform

### Option 3: DIY Recording
- Record yourself (authentic, free)
- Ask friends/family with good voices
- Edit with free tools (Audacity, DaVinci Resolve)
- Process with voice effects for character variation

### Option 4: Collaboration
- Partner with other modders for skill-sharing
- One person voices multiple characters
- Split credit and community recognition
